# Big Lever Institute curriculum

Curriculum snapshot: 2026-10-17.

Our free lessons help adults use AI for useful work while keeping their own judgment. Start Here is navigation, not a compulsory course. New learners can begin with AI Basics; experienced learners can choose a relevant task. Use 06-lesson-links.json for the recorded Skool destinations.

## Mini-courses

- Everyday AI — Practical Examples: Apply the basics to a personally useful email or résumé, then check and improve it.
- Startup Foundations: Develop a customer-grounded problem hypothesis and a useful next investigation.
- Digital Marketing: Turn a supported offer into an honest message and an informative test.
- AI Tools and Workflows: Produce a source-grounded synthesis that helps a particular audience make sense of a topic.
- Coding for Shippers: Make a small reversible software change and prepare it for review.
- MC6 — Prompting and Workflows: Preserve a method that proved useful, then adapt it without losing its limits.
- Product Thinking: Connect stakeholder evidence to a bounded scope and a defensible first priority.
