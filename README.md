# Your Big Lever AI Guides — Version 1

Choose the help you need. Both guides are free from Big Lever Institute.
Native Claude plugins require a supported paid Claude plan and may be restricted
by your organization's settings. Institute lessons do not require a paid upgrade.

- **Big Lever — Guide Me:** your coach, research assistant and thought partner.
  Ask a question, bring an exercise or improve a draft. No intake is required.
- **Big Lever — Your Learning Path:** choose lessons around your goals and
  experience, understand why they fit, and adapt a plan you control.

## Add the guides in Claude

Keep your Institute lesson open. In Claude, choose **Show sidebar** at the top left
if its navigation is hidden. Open Customize → Plugins → Add plugin → Add marketplace
→ Add from a repository. Paste the URL of the public repository displaying this README,
choose **Use**, then **Sync**. Add either guide. If its details page opens, choose **Search** at the top
to return to your results, or **Plugins** if that is the return link shown.
Add the other guide if wanted.

If the marketplace is already added, choose **Cancel**, then search **Big Lever**
in Plugins. Open a guide and check that its details say **from bli-ai-guides**.
Choose **Add** for any missing guide.

Choose **New** in the sidebar and keep **Chat** selected, or open
https://claude.ai/new. Replace the bracketed text in a starter below with your own
task or goal, paste the whole message into the message box, and send it:

“Use the installed Big Lever Guide Me skill. My task or question: [describe what
you want help with]. Help me work through it and choose a useful next step.”

“Use the installed Big Lever Your Learning Path skill. My goal: [describe something
you want to learn or accomplish]. Help me build a small learning path around it.”

Claude also provides Skills controls; their visibility can vary by surface.
You do not need Claude Code, a terminal, API keys, or ZIP files.

Access can vary by plan, organization settings and device. If this option is
missing, tell us what you see and continue using Institute lessons in your
ordinary chat. This repository is
separate from Anthropic's reviewed public directory.

Try Guide Me: “I'm working through a Big Lever exercise. Help me with my next step.”
Try Your Learning Path: “Help me choose a useful learning path around my goals.”
Copy any plan or progress note into notes you control. Paste a short handoff into
the other guide when useful; neither guide automatically sees the other's chats.

## Curriculum and feedback

Curriculum snapshot: 2026-10-17. Package version: 1.2.3. The Institute
maintains one curriculum and generates these bundles from it. An installed copy
does not automatically read live curriculum changes; use a current guide release.
Proposed practice and future course suggestions are distinct from published lessons.

Version 1 is an invitation to use the guides and tell us what helps or gets in your
way. Send optional feedback to **help@bigleverinstitute.org**, subject
**Big Lever AI Guides Version 1 feedback**. Include your goal, which guide/platform
you used, what happened, and what you needed. Redact private information.
The guides do not automatically send your chats or feedback to the Institute.
